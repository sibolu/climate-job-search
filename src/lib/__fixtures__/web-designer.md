## Experience Cards

### C1: Redesign of a utility's customer portal
- **Situation:** Agency engagement for a regional electric utility whose bill-pay and outage pages had a 40% mobile bounce rate.
- **Actions:** Ran stakeholder interviews and usability tests, built a design system in Figma, designed responsive flows for billing, outage maps and rebate enrollment, handed off to an in-house React team.
- **Results:** Mobile bounce fell to 18%; rebate enrollment completions doubled in the first quarter after launch.
- **Skills:** UX research, design systems, responsive design, Figma, developer handoff
- **Excluded:** no

### C2: Design lead for a transit agency trip-planning app

- **Results:** App rating went from 2.9 to 4.4; the agency's WCAG AA audit passed with zero critical findings.
- **Skills:** product design, accessibility, data-heavy UI, cross-functional collaboration
- **Excluded:** no
