WORK

Senior Product Designer — Kettle & Vine (design agency), Chicago. 2021–present.
Lead designer on the customer portal redesign for a regional electric utility.
Their bill-pay and outage pages had a 40% mobile bounce rate and the call center
was absorbing the difference. I ran fourteen stakeholder interviews and five
rounds of moderated usability tests, built a Figma design system the utility
still runs on, and designed responsive flows for billing, the outage map and
rebate enrollment. Handed the system off to their in-house React team with
annotated specs and a component-by-component walkthrough. Mobile bounce fell to
18%. Rebate enrollment completions doubled in the first quarter after launch.

Design Lead (contract) — City of Lakeport Transit Authority digital team.
2019–2021, two-year engagement.
Owned design for the agency's trip-planning and real-time arrivals app. Worked
daily with the GTFS data engineers to figure out what the feed could honestly
promise and designed around the gaps. Ran accessibility audits every sprint and
shipped monthly. The app store rating went from 2.9 to 4.4 over the engagement,
and the agency's WCAG AA audit closed with zero critical findings.

Web Designer — Foldermill (B2B SaaS), Chicago. 2015–2019.
