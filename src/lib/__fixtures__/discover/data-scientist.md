### C1: Causal inference for pricing in a two-sided marketplace
- **Situation:** Pricing experiments at a two-sided marketplace were badly confounded from 2021 onward: sellers reacted to price changes within hours, so naive A/B splits leaked across arms and results could not be trusted for pricing decisions.
- **Actions:** Designed switchback and cluster-randomized experiments to handle the interference, built an internal causal-inference library covering doubly robust estimators, synthetic controls and sensitivity analysis, and reviewed experiment designs company-wide at roughly one a week.
- **Results:** Six teams now use the causal-inference library and pricing decisions moved onto the platform. A synthetic-control study on a proposed fee change caught a 4% GMV loss that the original difference-in-means analysis had scored as neutral, and the change was pulled.
- **Skills:** experiment design, causal inference, internal tooling, design review
- **Excluded:** no

### C2: Hierarchical forecasting for operations and finance
- **Situation:** From 2019 to 2021, served operations and finance teams on a forecasting team where model training ran off a laptop cron job.
- **Actions:** Built hierarchical time-series forecasts reconciled across region and category, owned the feature pipeline and the monitoring dashboards, and migrated model training onto a scheduled batch system with alerting.
- **Results:** Forecast error fell 22% year over year, and the models have run unattended for 18 months since.
- **Skills:** time-series forecasting, data pipelines, model monitoring, production migration
- **Excluded:** no

### C3: Doctoral evaluation of a residential energy-efficiency subsidy
- **Situation:** PhD in Economics completed in 2017, with a dissertation on the effect of a state residential energy-efficiency subsidy using linked administrative utility billing and rebate records.
- **Actions:** Applied difference-in-differences with staggered adoption and a regression-discontinuity design at the income eligibility cutoff, working on a panel of about 900k households.
- **Results:** Produced two published papers; one was cited in a state regulatory filing on program cost-effectiveness.
- **Skills:** program evaluation, quasi-experimental methods, administrative data analysis, policy analysis
- **Excluded:** no
