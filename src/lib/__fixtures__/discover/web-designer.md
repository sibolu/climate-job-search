### C1: Utility customer portal redesign
- **Situation:** At a Chicago design agency from 2021 onward, served as lead designer on a customer portal redesign for a regional electric utility whose bill-pay and outage pages carried a 40% mobile bounce rate, pushing the overflow onto the call center.
- **Actions:** Ran fourteen stakeholder interviews and five rounds of moderated usability tests, built a Figma design system, and designed responsive flows for billing, the outage map and rebate enrollment. Handed the system to the utility's in-house React team with annotated specs and a component-by-component walkthrough.
- **Results:** Mobile bounce fell from 40% to 18%, rebate enrollment completions doubled in the first quarter after launch, and the utility still runs on the design system that was delivered.
- **Skills:** UX research, design systems, developer handoff, responsive design, stakeholder interviews
- **Excluded:** no

### C2: Transit trip-planning and arrivals app
- **Situation:** On a two-year contract engagement from 2019 to 2021 with a city transit authority's digital team, owned design for the agency's trip-planning and real-time arrivals app, constrained by what the GTFS data feed could honestly support.
- **Actions:** Worked daily with GTFS data engineers to establish what the feed could reliably promise and designed around its gaps, ran accessibility audits every sprint, and shipped monthly releases.
- **Results:** App store rating rose from 2.9 to 4.4 over the engagement, and the agency's WCAG AA audit closed with zero critical findings.
- **Skills:** accessibility auditing, data-informed design, cross-functional collaboration, shipping cadence
- **Excluded:** no

### C3: B2B SaaS marketing web design
- **Situation:** Early-career marketing-side web design role at a B2B SaaS company in Chicago from 2015 to 2019, supporting the demand-generation team.
- **Actions:** Built landing page templates in Webflow for demand-gen, ran A/B tests on hero layouts and CTA placement, and maintained the template library.
- **Results:** Conversion gains were small and much of the work was template maintenance, but the role established a habit of shipping weekly.
- **Skills:** landing page design, A/B testing, Webflow templates
- **Excluded:** no
