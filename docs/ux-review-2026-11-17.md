# UX review: 2026-11-17

Visual review of the local app (desktop Chrome via the Playwright MCP, 1440×900,
light theme). No app code was changed. The test profile was built live from a
real resume with `try:cards` → `try:discover` → `try:queries` (all three stages
succeeded; no fixture was used). Content from that resume is referred to by ID
only (C1–C5 cards, F1–F6 fields, R1–R2 roles, Q1–Q3 queries).

Screenshots live in the gitignored `.playwright-mcp/` folder.

**Caveat on the rail.** The scripts skip elicitation, so the test profile has
no preferences. On every screen the rail therefore shows the first preference
question ("Let's start with what draws you to climate work…"). A real user
answers those questions before reaching Fields, so the rail's *content* on
screens 3–7 is a test artefact. Findings about the rail's *presence and
layout* still stand.

---

## Top themes and recommended direction

### 1. Two columns compete for "what do I do next". Make it one column.

The right rail holds the step that unlocks progress (the three preference
questions) while the button it unlocks ("Match fields") sits at the bottom of
the main column, about 1,900px down. The helper text has to explain the link
("Answer climate interests, seniority, retraining appetite in the assistant
rail first."). After that the rail has little to do: on Fields, Searches and
Feedback it is a 370px column of mostly empty space with a profile link in it.

**Direction:** run the main flow in one centred column and remove the
permanent rail.
- Ask the three preference questions in the main column, as their own short
  step between Highlights and Fields (or as a block directly under the
  highlights, next to the button they unlock).
- Show progress and the working log in the main column while a turn runs
  (where Pending already sits), not in a sidebar.
- Move "Your profile" to one header item next to the step nav.
- Keep the per-field "more on this field" question inside the expanded field
  card, where it is asked.

This also removes most of the duplication listed below: the rail is where the
second "Assistant" label, the third privacy line and the second profile entry
point come from.

### 2. Too much prose for a wary reader. Lead with evidence, hide the rest.

Highlights shows each card's full Situation / Action / Result text. That is
roughly 150–250 words per card and five cards in a masonry grid with holes.
Field cards then restate the same evidence three times: a "you would…"
paragraph, a row of skill chips, and in the expanded view, the "From your
highlights" bullets plus role blurbs that cite the cards again. Mid-career
readers who distrust AI fluff will skim all of it and trust none of it.

**Direction:** one line of claim, one line of proof, details on demand.
- A highlight card shows title, dates, the one-sentence result and skills.
  S/A/R opens on Edit (or a "Details" disclosure).
- A field card shows name, a one-line description, fit, and *which of your
  highlights* it draws on (by title, as links). Drop either the paragraph or
  the chips; they say the same thing.
- Cut the uppercase monospace micro-labels (SITUATION, FROM YOUR HIGHLIGHTS,
  ROLES TO LOOK AT, POTENTIAL EMPLOYERS, SOURCES, SELECTED ROLES, WHAT WAS
  OFF?, MARKED WRONG) down to the two or three that actually separate
  sections. Together with the mono query boxes they make the app read like a
  developer tool.

### 3. The interface speaks the system's language, not the user's.

Several strings expose internals or promise things that don't exist:
- Role blurbs cite cards by raw ID, in the form "C1's <skill> and C2's <project>…". The
  Highlights screen never shows IDs, so the user can't decode these.
- The Searches header shows an "F1" badge.
- "Add info in chat": there is no chat. The beta has no composer
  (`Rail.tsx` comment, technical-plan D42).
- The first preference question says "Tap one below to start, or type your
  own", but there is no text box to type into (`src/lib/elicit.ts:163`).
- "Sector move", "Adjacent role", "Examine", "assistant rail", "retraining
  appetite", "Matching fields · 5 of 5 in", "not copied yet".
- The profile drawer is raw `profile.md` in monospace on a dark panel.

**Direction:** one copy pass with a simple rule: if the user can't see it
elsewhere on screen, don't refer to it. Replace IDs with card titles (in the
prompt or at render time) and rename "chat" to what actually happens.
Show the profile as a readable summary, with the raw file behind "Export".

### 4. Fix the three things that look broken or contradict themselves.

- The field-card rating row (Interesting / Unsure / Not for me) renders as
  empty boxes, with "Not for me" wrapped one word per line and spilling out
  of the card. It is on every field card, so it's the first thing that makes
  the board look unfinished.
- Clicking "Wrong results" opens a feedback screen with "Wrong roles"
  preselected and "Good fit" offered as an option. The user has to choose
  twice, with different words each time.
- The LinkedIn query card says "MARKED WRONG" after the query had already
  been revised by `try:queries`. Nothing tells the user whether the query on
  screen is the old one or the replacement.

### 5. Searches is the payoff. Make it the strongest screen.

Searches already works best: one card per board, the query, "Copy query",
"Open LinkedIn", the email-alert disclosure and "Ran it?" in a clear order.
Push further:
- Rating should happen inline on the query card, without a separate screen.
  Expand the card in place with the reason chips and "Send and revise". That
  removes "Back to searches" and the half-empty Feedback page.
- After a revise, show what changed (old query struck through, one-line
  reason). Trust comes from seeing the system respond to feedback.
- Decide whether the primary action is "Copy query" or "Open on LinkedIn".
  The open links already carry the keywords, so for a first try "Open" is the
  quicker payoff. "Copy" matters for the alert set-up, which is the second
  step.

**What I would remove outright:** the permanent rail, the field rating row
(or make clear what it does), the rank numbers on field cards (order already
says it), the "ALPHA" badge next to an empty logo square, the "Potential
employers" / "Sources" split (merge them into one list of employer links), and
the separate Feedback screen.

## Per screen

### 1. First run: `../.playwright-mcp/01-first-run.png`

1. **Copy: "No account, nothing stored." is styled as a link but opens the
   profile drawer.** On an empty state the drawer shows an empty profile and
   an Import button. That works as a way back in for returning users, but the
   label promises an explanation. *Change:* label it "Nothing is stored on a
   server: how this works", or show one sentence inline and move Import to a
   separate "Have a saved profile? Import it" link. S. `FirstRun.tsx`
2. **Clutter: empty white square logo + "ALPHA" pill.** The square carries no
   information and looks like a broken image. *Change:* drop the square; keep
   the alpha marker only if it sets an expectation, as plain small text. S.
   `Header.tsx`
3. **Copy: Notes placeholder repeats the label** ("Notes" / "Notes:
   constraints, interests, what you don't want. Optional."). *Change:*
   placeholder "Constraints, interests, what you don't want (optional)". S.
   `FirstRun.tsx`
4. **Taste:** the headline ("Start from what you have done, not from the word
   'climate'.") and "About a minute. You can correct everything after." are
   exactly the right tone. Keep both.

### 2. Highlights: `../.playwright-mcp/02-highlights.png`

1. **Flow: the gating step and the button it unlocks are in different
   columns, a screen and a half apart.** "Match fields" is disabled at the very
   bottom; the questions that enable it are top-right. *Change:* see Theme 1.
   At minimum, make the button sticky and change its helper to "Answer the
   three questions on the right to continue." M. `Highlights.tsx`,
   `Rail.tsx`, `AppShell.tsx`
2. **Clutter: full S/A/R prose on every card.** Each card is a wall of text;
   the grid leaves large holes (column 3 ends halfway). *Change:* collapse to
   title, dates, the result line and skills; full S/A/R on Edit. Use a single
   column list instead of masonry. M. `Highlights.tsx`
3. **Copy: "Matching fields · 5 of 5 in"** is cryptic and set in a monospace
   badge. *Change:* "Using 5 of 5 highlights", plain text near the
   button. S. `Highlights.tsx:40`
4. **Copy: "Answer climate interests, seniority, retraining appetite in the
   assistant rail first."** Jargon ("retraining appetite", "assistant rail").
   *Change:* as in finding 1. S. `Highlights.tsx`
5. **Duplicate info: "Assistant" appears twice in the rail** (column header
   and the message's label). *Change:* drop the message label. S. `Rail.tsx`
6. **Copy: the first question says "or type your own"** but there is no
   input. *Change:* "Tap one below. 'Not sure yet' is a good answer." Shorter,
   and true. S. `src/lib/elicit.ts:163`
7. **Duplicate info: privacy reassurance in the rail** ("Everything known
   about you, kept on this device.") repeats the first-run line and the
   drawer's line. Once per screen is enough; the header is the place. S.
   `Rail.tsx`

### 3. Field board, collapsed: `../.playwright-mcp/03-field-board.png`

1. **Clutter / broken: the Interesting / Unsure / Not for me row.** Empty
   square boxes, labels offset below them, "Not for me" wrapped one word per
   line and overflowing the card bottom on every card. *Change:* fix the
   layout, and decide whether the row earns its place (Theme 5). If it stays,
   put it only in the expanded card and explain what it does ("Not for me
   hides this field and tells the next ranking"). S to fix, M to rethink.
   `FieldCard.tsx` (status row)
2. **Duplicate info: paragraph and skill chips say the same thing.** Each
   card has a two-sentence "You would…" paragraph and four chips that restate
   the skills it names. *Change:* keep one. For evidence, I'd keep a short
   "Draws on: <highlight title>, <highlight title>" line, which ties the claim
   to the user's own cards. M. `FieldCard.tsx`
3. **Duplicate CTA: "Click on a card to see more info and roles." + "See
   Roles" on every card.** Both say the same thing; the whole card is already
   the button. *Change:* drop the subtitle; keep "See roles" (sentence case).
   S. `FieldBoard.tsx`, `FieldCard.tsx:107`
4. **Copy: "Sector move" / "Adjacent role".** These are the transition type,
   but nothing says so and the words are internal. *Change:* "Same work, new
   sector" / "A neighbouring role", or drop it and let the one-line
   description carry it. S. `FieldCard.tsx`
5. **Clutter: rank numbers (1–6) in boxes.** Order already conveys rank; the
   boxed numbers add a third visual element to every header row. S.
   `FieldCard.tsx`
6. **Taste:** "Strong overlap" / "Some overlap" with a small bar is the right
   level of confidence language. Keep it.

### 4. Field board, F1 expanded: `../.playwright-mcp/04-field-expanded.png`

1. **Copy: role blurbs cite cards by raw ID** (both R1 and R2 read like "C1's <skill>
   and C2's <project> transfer to…"). The user
   never sees C-numbers anywhere else. This is the biggest trust leak in the
   app: evidence the user can't trace. *Change:* have the discovery prompt cite
   by short card title, or substitute titles at render time. S–M.
   `FieldCard.tsx`, discovery prompt in `src/lib/`
2. **Duplicate info: the same evidence in three places.** Skill chips, "From
   your highlights" (three card titles with a one-line reason each) and the
   role blurbs (citing the same cards again). *Change:* keep "From your
   highlights" (it's the best evidence block in the app) and make the role
   blurbs about the role, not the cards. M. `FieldCard.tsx`
3. **Duplicate CTA/instructions: the same instruction three times.** "Select
   the ones you want searches for." under ROLES TO LOOK AT, "Tick the roles
   you want searched." beside the button, and the disabled button itself reads
   "Select a role first". *Change:* keep the button label, which updates with
   state; drop both helper lines. S. `FieldCard.tsx`
4. **Flow / Copy: "Add info in chat".** There is no chat. The button opens a
   question in the rail. *Change:* ask the question inline in the "What is
   uncertain" box with its own small input, labelled "Answer this". M.
   `FieldCard.tsx:43`, `Rail.tsx`
5. **Duplicate info: "Potential employers" chips and "Sources" URLs** list
   largely the same companies. Raw URLs as link text add noise. *Change:* one
   "Employers to look at" list, each name linking to its careers page. S.
   `FieldCard.tsx`
6. **Clutter: "What is uncertain" box is half empty** next to the taller
   roles box, and amber-bordered, so it pulls the eye away from the role
   checkboxes. *Change:* keep the content (honesty about uncertainty builds
   trust), but put it as a short note under the roles at normal weight. S.
   `FieldCard.tsx`
7. **Flow: the primary button ("Select a role first" → write searches) comes
   before the rating row**, which then sits under it, still broken. The last
   thing in the card should be the next step. S. `FieldCard.tsx`
8. **Flow: expanding F1 reflows every other card** (the grid switches from 2
   columns to a full-width card, then 2 columns again), so the user loses
   their place. *Change:* open the field as a detail view or a side panel in
   the main column, or keep a one-column list throughout. M. `FieldBoard.tsx`

### 5. Profile drawer open: `../.playwright-mcp/05-profile-drawer.png`

1. **Taste / Clutter: the profile is shown as raw markdown** (`# Profile`,
   `## Preferences`, `- **Years:**…`) in monospace on a dark green panel. For a
   wary non-technical user this reads as a debug console, not "everything we
   know about you". *Change:* render the profile readably (preferences,
   highlights by title, excluded items, fields), with the raw file available
   via Export. M. `ProfileDrawer.tsx`
2. **Clutter: "Export .json" is the only filled button**, so the most
   prominent action in the drawer is a file export. *Change:* make all four
   neutral; keep "Delete everything" visually distinct (red outline is
   fine). S. `ProfileDrawer.tsx`
3. **Duplicate info: three privacy lines on screen at once.** The drawer's
   "Stored on this device only. Never on a server." + the "About this data"
   info button + the rail's "Everything known about you, kept on this
   device." *Change:* one line in the drawer; nothing in the rail. S.
   `ProfileDrawer.tsx`, `Rail.tsx`
4. **Copy: "Your Profile" (rail) vs "Your profile" (drawer); "Examine".**
   *Change:* sentence case everywhere; "View" instead of "Examine". S.
   `Rail.tsx:77`
5. **Flow: the drawer pushes the page down rather than overlaying it**, and
   is opened from the rail, far from the header where users look for account
   or data controls. *Change:* open it from a header item. S–M.
   `Header.tsx`, `AppShell.tsx`

### 6. Searches: `../.playwright-mcp/06-searches.png`

1. **Flow: "MARKED WRONG" on a query that has already been revised.** In
   this profile Q1 (LinkedIn) was revised after a scripted "bad" rating; the
   card still says "MARKED WRONG" and offers Good / Wrong again, with no hint
   that the text is new. *Change:* after a revise, show the new query as
   current, mark the status "Revised. Try it again", and offer the old one
   behind a disclosure. M. `QueryCard.tsx:43`
2. **Duplicate CTA: "Back to fields" + the "2 Fields" step button** in the
   header, a few hundred pixels apart. *Change:* drop "Back to fields"; the
   step nav is enough. S. `Searches.tsx`
3. **Copy: "F1" badge in the field header.** Internal ID. *Change:* remove.
   S. `Searches.tsx`
4. **Duplicate CTA (soft): "Copy query" (filled, primary) and "Open
   LinkedIn" (link).** The Open link already carries the keywords, so for a
   first try it is the faster route. The subtitle "Copy one, run it in your
   own tab" describes the slower path. *Change:* make "Open on LinkedIn"
   primary and "Copy" secondary, and reword the subtitle to "Open one, check
   the results, then tell us how it went." S. `QueryCard.tsx`, `Searches.tsx`
5. **Clutter: queries in monospace grey boxes.** Fine for the boolean
   strings, but the Climatebase query is four plain words and looks equally
   technical. Minor. S. `QueryCard.tsx`
6. **Taste:** card order (query → copy/open → email alert → "Ran it?") is
   right, and "Turn it into an email alert" is the most useful line in the
   product. Keep it.

### 7. Feedback (after "Wrong results" on the Indeed card): `../.playwright-mcp/07-feedback.png`

1. **Flow / Copy: the choice is asked twice with different words.** The card
   button says "Wrong results"; the next screen preselects "Wrong roles" and
   still offers "Good fit". *Change:* do the whole thing inline on the query
   card (Theme 5). If it stays a screen, carry the user's choice through
   unchanged and use the same four labels on both. M. `QueryCard.tsx`,
   `Feedback.tsx`
2. **Copy: "not copied yet"** appended to the query in the subtitle reads as
   a reproach and exposes tracking state. *Change:* drop it. S. `Feedback.tsx`
3. **Copy: "Revises the query, the ranking and your profile."** A wary user
   will not want a search rating to change their profile without knowing how.
   *Change:* "Rewrites this search. You'll see the change and can keep the
   old one." (accept-or-keep already exists in `screens.ts`). S. `Feedback.tsx`
4. **Duplicate CTA: "Back to searches" + the active "3 Searches" step
   button.** Also wraps onto two lines. S. `Feedback.tsx`
5. **Clutter: four options in a three-column grid** leave "No results"
   orphaned on its own row, and the panel fills half the width, leaving the
   right half of the main column empty. S. `Feedback.tsx`
6. **Taste:** the reason chips ("Too senior", "Too junior", "Wrong
   discipline", "Needs a licence", "Wrong location") are well chosen and
   faster than typing. Keep them.

## What is working

- First-run headline and the "About a minute. You can correct everything
  after." helper: calm, honest, specific.
- Step nav (1 Highlights / 2 Fields / 3 Searches) with later steps disabled
  until reachable.
- Plain fit language: "Strong overlap" / "Some overlap".
- "From your highlights" in the expanded field card: evidence from the user's
  own experience, one line each.
- "What is uncertain": admitting what the app doesn't know is the strongest
  trust signal in the product. Keep the content, quieten the box.
- Query cards: board name, query, copy/open, "Turn it into an email alert",
  "Ran it?" in that order.
- Feedback reason chips.
- Restrained colour palette; one accent colour used for primary actions.

## Not covered

- **Pending screen** (needs a live model call; the server ran with the API
  key disabled on purpose).
- **The rail after the three questions are answered**: the test profile has
  no preferences (the scripts skip elicitation), so the rail showed the first
  question on every screen.
- Edit mode and the excluded state of a highlight card.
- The "Add info in chat" live state and the per-field question in the rail.
- Accept-or-keep after a revise, and any error or parser-warning states.
- Searches for more than one field (only F1 had queries).

## Privacy

Resume-derived content is referred to only by ID (C1–C5, F1–F6, R1–R2,
Q1–Q3) and generic descriptions. The test profiles, session files,
snapshots and screenshots stay in the gitignored `.playwright-mcp/` folder.
