### ISCO-08 (International Standard Classification of Occupations, ILO)

- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/public/english/bureau/stat/isco/isco08/index.htm
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-23
- **What it is:** The ILO's occupation classification, used as the common bridge: ESCO ties each occupation to one ISCO-08 unit group, and the US SOC Policy Committee approved an ISCO-08 to SOC 2010 crosswalk in July 2012. https://www.bls.gov/soc/ISCO_SOC_Crosswalk_process.pdf
- **Geography:** global.
- **Granularity:** occupation codes; number of rows and level count not confirmed (ILOSTAT page returned 403). https://ilostat.ilo.org/methods/concepts-and-definitions/classification-occupation/
- **Last update and cadence:** ISCO-08 (2008); ILO also publishes ISCO-88 to ISCO-08 correspondence tables. https://www.ilo.org/resource/correspondence-table-isco88-isco08 A newer revision: not found.
- **Format and licence:** ILO spreadsheets and web; licence not found. ESCO states its ISCO information is based on ILO work. https://esco.ec.europa.eu/ar/node/203
- **Trades vs professional skew:** balanced (general classification, all occupations); no green flag.
- **US–EU occupation crosswalk:** ISCO-08 to SOC 2010 exists (BLS) https://www.bls.gov/soc/ISCO_SOC_Crosswalk_process.pdf ; ESCO to ISCO-08 exists https://esco.ec.europa.eu/ar/node/203 . A direct ESCO-to-SOC table was not found. The BLS crosswalk targets SOC 2010; mapping to SOC 2018 not confirmed.

### OECD green jobs and skills work (OECD)

- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-employment-outlook-2024_ac8b3538-en/full-report/component-6.html
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-23
- **What it is:** OECD Employment Outlook 2024 chapter and working papers measuring green-driven occupations using O*NET-style task inventories.
- **Geography:** OECD countries; "Doing green things" also covers many OECD and non-OECD EU countries. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/doing-green-things-skills-reallocation-and-the-green-transition_286a5007-en.html
- **Granularity:** occupations scored by green tasks (US via O*NET); row count not found. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-employment-outlook-2024_ac8b3538-en/full-report/component-6.html
- **Last update and cadence:** Employment Outlook 2024; working paper ECO/WKP(2023)16; one-off analyses. https://one.oecd.org/document/ECO/WKP%282023%2916/en/pdf
- **Format and licence:** web and PDF; licence not found. (Pages returned 403 to the fetch tool; facts from search summaries.)
