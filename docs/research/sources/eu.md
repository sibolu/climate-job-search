### ESCO–O*NET crosswalk (European Commission)

- **URL:** https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/other-crosswalks
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-23
- **What it is:** An official mapping table from O*NET occupations to ESCO occupations.
- **Geography:** both
- **Granularity:** One row per O*NET occupation mapped to an ESCO occupation; row count not found. https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/other-crosswalks
- **Last update and cadence:** Technical report published 2022-09-30 (https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/publications/publication/crosswalk-between-esco-and-onet-technical-report); file path suggests an upload in 2023-08, no version or date stated on the page. https://esco.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2023-08/ONET_%28Occupations%29_0_updated.csv

### Environmental goods and services sector accounts, EGSS (Eurostat)

- **URL:** https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_egss_esms.htm
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-23
- **What it is:** National accounts-style data on output, exports, value added and employment of the environmental goods and services sector.
- **Geography:** EU (member-state data plus Eurostat EU aggregates)
- **Granularity:** One row is a country-year-activity value; breakdown by environmental domain (CEPA/CReMA); NACE breakdown not confirmed. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_egss_esms.htm
- **Last update and cadence:** Employment series 2000-2022 in the 2025 article (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php/Environmental_economy_-_employment_and_growth); newer table code env_egss_emp cited in a 2026 article, not confirmed against the database.
- **Format and licence:** Eurostat database tables; licence not found on the pages read. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_egss_esms.htm
- **Trades vs professional skew:** not found; data is industry-level, not occupations. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_egss_esms.htm
- **US–EU occupation crosswalk:** not applicable; no occupation codes. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_egss_esms.htm

### EU Taxonomy for sustainable activities, activity list (European Commission)

- **URL:** https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-23
- **What it is:** The Commission's list of economic activities that can contribute to environmental objectives; each activity lists NACE codes, but the Commission treats them as indicative only and the activity description defines scope. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52022XC1006(01) *(corrected in spot check, 2026-11-23)*
- **Geography:** EU
- **Granularity:** One row is a taxonomy activity with NACE codes and objective flags; count not found. https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/
- **Last update and cadence:** Not found. The Navigator is at https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/ (an older finance.ec.europa.eu URL returned 404); NACE Rev. 2.1 applies from 2025 so some mirrors use older codes. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nace
- **Format and licence:** Commission web tool, the EU Taxonomy Navigator https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/ (an API endpoint at webgate.ec.europa.eu/sft returned HTTP 500 on 2026-11-23); licence not found.
- **Trades vs professional skew:** not found; sector and industry level, so it skews professional/neutral by design but this is unconfirmed. https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/
- **US–EU occupation crosswalk:** not applicable; NACE industry codes only. https://ec.europa.eu/sustainable-finance-taxonomy/
- **Coverage of the ten areas:**

  | Area | Coverage | Evidence |
  |---|---|---|
  | CE | partial | circular economy is one of six objectives per https://generationimpact.global/tools/taxonomy-compass/ |
  | BE | partial | activities include construction and real estate (NACE F, L); mirror table at https://generationimpact.global/tools/taxonomy-compass/ |
  | TR | partial | transport NACE 49-53 grouped as in https://www.finexus.uzh.ch/en/projects/EU-Taxonomy-Alignement.html |
  | EN | partial | utilities NACE 35.11-35.13 per https://www.finexus.uzh.ch/en/projects/EU-Taxonomy-Alignement.html |
  | FA | partial | forestry (NACE A2) afforestation in https://zenodo.org/records/5791921/files/Tidy_EU_Taxonomy.pdf |
  | CR | partial | Climate annex has 5.11 Transport of CO2, 5.12 Underground permanent geological storage of CO2, 9.2 R&D for direct air capture. https://ec.europa.eu/finance/docs/level-2-measures/taxonomy-regulation-delegated-act-2021-2800-annex-1_en.pdf *(corrected in spot check, 2026-11-23)* |
  | CF | partial | Adaptation annex section 10 'Financial and insurance activities': 10.1 Non-life insurance underwriting of climate-related perils, 10.2 Reinsurance. https://ec.europa.eu/finance/docs/level-2-measures/taxonomy-regulation-delegated-act-2021-2800-annex-2_en.pdf *(corrected in spot check, 2026-11-23)* |
  | IM | partial | manufacturing activities (C26, C27) per https://generationimpact.global/tools/taxonomy-compass/ |
  | NW | partial | water and biodiversity objectives per https://generationimpact.global/tools/taxonomy-compass/ |
  | CS | partial | Adaptation annex 9.1 Engineering activities and related technical consultancy dedicated to adaptation to climate change. https://ec.europa.eu/finance/docs/level-2-measures/taxonomy-regulation-delegated-act-2021-2800-annex-2_en.pdf *(corrected in spot check, 2026-11-23)* |

- **Notes:**
- Strong candidate for industry areas as a NACE-keyed list; the official page was not reachable, so figures rest on mirrors.
- ESRS sector classification is a different NACE grouping and not interchangeable (https://efrag.org/system/files/sites/webpublishing/Meeting%20Documents/2404191339179145/04-03%20-%20Sector%20classification%20SEC%201%20-%20compared%20to%20version%202%20March%202023%20-%20SR%20TEG%20240508.pdf).
